# Medical content rules

## Sources
WHO guidance, then the national health authority of each target market, then national clinical guidelines and peer-reviewed research. Record source, version date, and the claim it supports.

## Status labels (only these; public shows only truthful status)
Draft → Under Review → Medically Reviewed → Published → Needs Review → Archived.

## Workflow
Draft → editorial review → medical review → approved → published → scheduled review. Only authorised roles approve and publish.

## Rules
- Every clinical statement is marked "pending qualified reviewer" until a reviewer signs off.
- No invented reviewers, credentials, citations, or statistics.
- AI-assisted text cannot be published as reviewed without human review.
