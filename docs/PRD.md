# PRD

## Goal
Trustworthy, plain-language sexual and reproductive health education, organised as an A–Z index over reviewed articles.

## Audience
General public, men, women, couples, students, healthcare professionals. Adolescents: content gated until a safeguarding and legal review is complete.

## In scope (MVP)
- A–Z index linking to one canonical article per topic
- About 100–150 reviewed entries
- Search (PostgreSQL full-text), glossary, source links per claim
- Medical disclaimer, privacy, terms, contact, editorial policy pages
- Report-an-error form (no personal data stored beyond the message)

## Out of scope (launch)
AI assistant, community or Q&A, telehealth, advertising, user accounts, adolescent content, clinical decision tools, fertility/ovulation estimators (post-review).

## MVP acceptance
- Every published article shows reviewer, review date, version, and sources
- Drafts and unreviewed content are never public
- Works on mobile, keyboard-navigable, WCAG 2.2 AA target
